GatiShakti · Geospatial Business Intelligence Platform UX/UI redesign

Context: Adani’s geospatial decision-support platform combining location, infrastructure and business information to help teams assess regions and identify expansion opportunities.

My role: led the UX/UI redesign of the existing platform to improve usability and reduce complexity.

  • Evaluated the existing experience to identify usability issues, visual complexity and friction in key tasks.
  • Simplified the interface and established clearer information hierarchy for map-heavy, data-intensive screens.
  • Redesigned workflows for business and engineering users with different information needs.
  • Focused the design on turning complex geospatial and business data into clearer, actionable information.

DRS (Digital Routing System) Enterprise B2B · Supply Chain Management Platform · SAP-integrated

Led the product design direction and end-to-end UX/UI for a supply chain management platform supporting solar and wind construction operations.

  • Worked with BAs, developers and client stakeholders across 4 business functions, aligning business requirements, technical constraints and user needs.
  • Co-defined the To-Be business process across 8 project stages with the BA and supported client alignment and sign-off.
  • Shaped the information architecture, role-based access model and role configurator supporting 16 user roles.
  • Designed workflows across service orders, BoQ and drawing management, plant-map work allocation, material requisitions, inventory checks, approvals, quality and measurement certificates.
  • Worked with developers to understand SAP integration and system dependencies and translate backend processes into usable front-end experiences.
  • Established reusable UI components, colour tokens and light/dark themes to improve consistency and scalability.
  • Directed data-dense dashboards and 14 reports/KPIs, with clickable prototypes for stakeholder reviews and decision-making.
  • Led user testing and design iterations, incorporating client feedback and guiding developer handoff.
  • Outcome: Product is live in production, replacing paper-based tracking with a unified digital platform; some backend integrations remain in progress.

Pulse · Request for Inspection (RFI) Quality inspection & vendor billing

Designed the end-to-end RFI workflow connecting vendor submission, site-engineer inspection, approval/rejection, status tracking and invoice-payment release.

  • Outcome: Live in production and transitioned to maintenance.

HOTO · Handover / Takeover to Operations Plant commissioning

Designed the plant handover and cross-verification workflow, including inter-team checks, approvals and sign-offs required before operational commissioning and grid connection.

  • Outcome: Live in production and transitioned to maintenance.
